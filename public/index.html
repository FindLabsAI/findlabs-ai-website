What is an AI employee?

A business-ready system, not a generic chatbot.

An AI employee is a company-specific software system that combines AI models, your business knowledge, workflow rules, integrations, permissions, and human approval.

Generic AI

  • General-purpose assistance
  • Manual copy and paste
  • Limited company context by default
  • Does not automatically implement business workflows
  • Requires users to operate it manually

FindLabs AI Employee

  • Built for a specific organization
  • Connected to approved systems
  • Uses company knowledge
  • Executes defined workflows
  • Enforces permissions and approvals
  • Provides monitoring and auditability
  • Delivered and supported by FindLabs AI
GPT is the engine. FindLabs AI builds the car.

Example workflows

AI employees for real operating work.

Sales Follow-up Employee

Reads inboxes, checks CRM status, drafts approved follow-ups, updates Salesforce or HubSpot, and records actions.

Customer Support Employee

Classifies tickets, uses knowledge bases, drafts replies, escalates exceptions, and updates support systems.

Document Intelligence Employee

Extracts structured details, summarizes documents, routes reviews, and maintains audit records.

HR and Recruiting Employee

Coordinates candidate workflows, screens against approved criteria, drafts updates, and schedules next steps.

Operations Employee

Automates recurring internal workflows across tools, rules, approvals, notifications, and reports.

Research Employee

Researches products, vendors, competitors, and market signals using defined sources and reporting formats.

Illustrative healthcare workflow

A healthcare organization could ask an AI employee to summarize arriving lab reports, notify authorized staff, update approved workflow systems, and schedule follow-up actions when required by organization-specific rules. This is an illustrative administrative workflow, not a claim of medical diagnosis or fully autonomous clinical decision-making.
